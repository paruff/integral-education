# Discovery Brief: EI-22 — Pilot: Interpersonal Line suite

## Job to Be Done

When a program facilitator needs to validate the Interpersonal Line module suite (EI-08 through EI-11 plus EI-07 integration capstone), they want a structured pilot runbook with Kegan stage-based participant criteria, cross-difference safety protocols, and perspective-taking accuracy instruments, so they can run a safe, rigorous 8-week pilot with 15-20 diverse participants that produces actionable findings for module revision.

## Riskiest Assumption

The Interpersonal Line module sequence, cross-difference safety protocols, and trust-building practices can be executed by 15-20 diverse-stage and diverse-identity participants over 8 weeks without producing harm from identity threat, minority stress, or conflict material re-activation, and with measurable improvement in perspective-taking accuracy. If cross-difference dialogue produces harm or trust-building fails in a diverse cohort, the suite needs significant revision before public release.

## Acceptance Criterion

Given `docs/pilots/pilot-interpersonal-line.md`, when a qualified facilitator with cross-cultural dialogue experience reviews it, then they find: pilot objectives for the 4-module Interpersonal suite, participant criteria with Kegan stage diversity targets (30% Institutional, 40% Institutional/Interindividual boundary, 30% Interindividual) and deliberate social identity diversity recruitment, consent/screening with EI-20 Safety Addendum questions for conflict material reflection and cross-difference dialogue, facilitation requirements with cross-cultural experience and clinical backup for EI-10 and EI-11, data collection instruments (pre/post perspective-taking accuracy using IRI or equivalent, trust rating in cohort relationships, weekly practice log, safety incident log, weeks 4 and 8 qualitative debriefs), an 8-week timeline, revision triggers, and explicit Tier 2 safety protocol activation for EI-10 and EI-11.

## DORA Outcome Target

- Capability: User-centric focus
- Metric: Pilot completion rate and pre/post perspective-taking improvement
- Current baseline: Not applicable — first Interpersonal Line pilot
- Target: Establish baseline metrics; determine whether module sequence and cross-difference dialogue practices are valid for public release
- Measurement: Pilot data collection instruments

## Prior Art

- `docs/pilots/pilot-emotional-line.md` (EI-21) — created in this same session, provides the structural template
- EI-21 and EI-22 are companion pilots that together validate the Emotional and Interpersonal lines

## Notes

- The Interpersonal suite is smaller than the Emotional suite (4 core modules vs 7), but the practices carry higher relational risk due to cross-difference dialogue and conflict material reflection
- Kegan stage language (Institutional, Interindividual) is used for participant criteria rather than the color labels used in EI-21 — this reflects the Interpersonal line's specific grounding in constructive-developmental theory
- The EI-20 Safety Addendum specifically covers EI-10 (conflict material reflection) and EI-11 (cross-difference dialogue) in its Tier 2 trigger table
- Deliberate social identity diversity is specified in the issue — this is distinct from stage diversity and requires separate recruitment attention
