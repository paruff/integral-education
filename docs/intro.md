## 🌐 Integral Education Platform

Welcome to the **Integral Education Platform** — a mastery-based learning system built on the <Term term="AQAL" definition="All Quadrants, All Levels, All Lines, All States, All Types — the comprehensive map of human experience and development." /> framework (All <Term term="Quadrants" definition="The four fundamental perspectives: I (subjective), It (behavioural), We (cultural), Its (systemic)." />, All <Term term="Levels/Stages" definition="Distinct levels of development, each with its own worldview — e.g., Amber → Orange → Green → Teal." />, All <Term term="Lines" definition="Specific developmental domains: cognitive, emotional, interpersonal, moral, somatic, spiritual, and self." />, All <Term term="States" definition="Temporary conditions of consciousness: waking, dreaming, deep sleep, and nondual awareness." />, All <Term term="Types" definition="Relatively stable characteristics like personality style, learning preference, and cultural orientation." />).

## What is Integral Education?

Integral Education is a comprehensive approach to human development that honors:

- **All <Term term="Quadrants" definition="The four fundamental perspectives in AQAL: Interior-Individual, Exterior-Individual, Interior-Collective, Exterior-Collective." />**: Interior/Exterior × Individual/Collective
- **All <Term term="Levels/Stages" definition="Developmental stages unfold in sequence — each represents a coherent worldview with distinct strengths and limitations." />**: Developmental stages from Archaic to Integral
- **All <Term term="Lines" definition="Lines of development grow through stages independently. Cognitive, emotional, interpersonal, somatic, and more." />**: Cognitive, emotional, interpersonal, somatic, and more
- **All <Term term="States" definition="Temporary states of consciousness — gross (waking), subtle (dreaming), causal (formless), nondual (unity)." />**: Mindfulness, flow, peak experiences, and ordinary awareness
- **All <Term term="Types" definition="Stable individual differences like personality, learning preferences, and cultural orientations that cut across stages." />**: Personality styles, learning preferences, and cultural orientations

## 🧭 How to Use This Platform

### <Term term="Mastery Loop" definition="The five-step learning cycle: Learn → Practice → Reflect → Assess → Integrate. Designed to move knowledge into embodied capability." />

Every module follows the same learning cycle:

1. **🧠 Learn** — High-signal concept explanation
2. **🧘 Practice** — Embodied exercise (10–20 min)
3. **🔍 Reflect** — Guided self-inquiry
4. **📊 Assess** — Self-assessment rubric
5. **🔗 Integrate** — Real-life application
