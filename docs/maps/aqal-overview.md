## 🗺️ AQAL Overview

**AQAL** = All Quadrants, All Levels, All Lines, All States, All Types

Developed by Ken Wilber, AQAL is the most comprehensive map of human experience and development available. It is a *map* — not the territory — but a useful orienting framework.

---

## Where (Quadrants)

The four quadrants divide reality into four irreducible perspectives:

|  | **Individual** | **Collective** |
|---|---|---|
| **Interior** | **I** — Subjective experience, consciousness, intention | **We** — Intersubjective culture, shared values, worldview |
| **Exterior** | **It** — Objective behavior, brain states, observable action | **Its** — Interobjective systems, social structures, institutions |

All four are real and necessary. Reducing reality to only one quadrant is a **partial perspective** (e.g., materialism = only the "It" quadrant).

---

## Depth (Levels)

Levels represent stages of increasing complexity and capacity. Key levels (using color-coded spiral dynamics notation):

| Color | Level | Worldview |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Beige | Archaic | Instinctive survival |
| Purple | Magic | Tribal, animistic |
| Red | Power | Egocentric, impulsive |
| Amber | Mythic | Rule-based, conventional |
| Orange | Rational | Achievement, science, autonomy |
| Green | Pluralistic | Egalitarian, multicultural |
| Teal | Integral | Systemic, multi-perspectival |
| Turquoise | Super-Integral | Global, holistic |

Development is *transcend and include* — each level includes and goes beyond the previous.

---

## Skills (Lines)

Lines are relatively independent developmental streams:

- **Cognitive** — What can I know?
- **Emotional** — What do I feel?
- **Moral** — What is right?
- **Interpersonal** — How do I relate?
- **Somatic** — How do I inhabit my body?
- **Spiritual** — What is the nature of existence?

A person can be at different levels on different lines (e.g., high cognitive, low emotional).

---

## Experience (States)

States are temporary experiences of consciousness:

- **Waking** — Ordinary gross reality
- **Dreaming** — Subtle states (imagery, emotion, vision)
- **Deep sleep** — Causal states (formless, empty)
- **Witnessing** — Turiya: pure awareness
- **Non-dual** — Subject/object collapse

**Practice** can stabilize access to higher states.

---

## Style (Types)

Types are stable styles that cut across levels, lines, and states:

- **Masculine/Feminine** principles (present in all genders)
- **Personality types** (e.g., MBTI, Enneagram)
- **Cultural types** (e.g., Eastern/Western orientations)

---

## How to Use AQAL

When facing a challenge, ask:
1. **Quadrant check**: Am I addressing all four quadrants?
2. **Level check**: What developmental level is this person or system operating from?
3. **Line check**: Which developmental line is most relevant here?
4. **State check**: What state of consciousness is most supportive for this work?
5. **Type check**: How do different personality types approach this situation?

## Optional 4-Path Lens

Use this lens when it improves learner accessibility:

- **Growing Up**: developmental complexity and perspective-taking
- **Waking Up**: awareness and state training
- **Cleaning Up**: shadow integration and trigger repair
- **Showing Up**: ethical participation and systems contribution
