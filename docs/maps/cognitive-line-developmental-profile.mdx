## Cognitive Line Developmental Profile Assessment

This interactive tool helps you locate your cognitive operations across the developmental spectrum — from concrete operational reasoning through formal, postformal, and early metasystematic thinking. Unlike self-report assessments of other developmental lines, cognitive operations can be revealed through performance on structured tasks.

The assessment has three sections:

- **Section A (Cognitive Operations Tasks):** 8 graduated reasoning problems spanning the Commons Model of Hierarchical Complexity spectrum. Select the response that best represents how you would approach each problem.
- **Section B (Dialectical Thinking):** 4 open-ended problems requiring dialectical responses. After writing, evaluate your response against Basseches' scoring criteria.
- **Section C (Metacognitive Reflection):** 5 questions about your experience of completing the assessment — the way you reflect on your own thinking is itself a window into your cognitive development.

At the end, you will receive a cognitive operations profile, dialectical thinking self-assessment, and personalised module recommendations. No data is stored or transmitted.
