## Moral Line Developmental Profile Assessment

This interactive tool helps you explore **how** you reason through moral dilemmas — not just what positions you hold, but the structure of your moral thinking. It adapts the Defining Issues Test (DIT) methodology (Rest, 1979; Rest et al., 1999), which has been validated with over 50,000 participants, for self-guided developmental exploration.

The assessment has three sections:

- **Section A (Justice Track):** Three moral dilemmas adapted from the DIT tradition. For each, you make a binary decision and then rate the importance of different considerations. Your pattern of ratings reveals which of Rest's three moral schemas — Personal Interest, Maintaining Norms, or Postconventional — you draw on most.
- **Section B (Care Track):** Two dilemmas designed to surface care-based moral reasoning — attention to relationships, context, and the wellbeing of specific others. This track addresses Gilligan's (1982) critique that Kohlberg's original model focused exclusively on justice.
- **Section C (Moral Courage Gap):** Five questions exploring the gap between what you believe is right and what you actually do — the Narvaez and Rest (1995) action gap.

At the end, you will receive a schema profile, care orientation score, moral courage gap assessment, and personalised module recommendations. No data is stored or transmitted.
