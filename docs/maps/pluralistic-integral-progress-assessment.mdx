## Pluralistic → Integral Stage Progress Self-Assessment

This assessment is designed for learners who have been working with pluralistic (Green) frameworks and are beginning to notice signs of movement toward integral (Teal) development. It uses **behavioural and phenomenological scenarios** — asking what you notice yourself doing, not what you believe — to reduce the self-report inflation that Cook-Greuter's research consistently identifies at this stage.

The assessment is divided into four sections:

- **Section A** — Late-Green Signals Recognition (8 questions)
- **Section B** — Vision-Logic & Metasystematic Thinking (6 cognitive-task questions)
- **Section C** — Integral Shadow Detection (10 questions probing known Teal shadow patterns)
- **Section D** — Cross-Line Embodiment Profile (8 questions across emotional, somatic, relational, and moral lines)

Results include a developmental profile across all four sections, named growing edges, personalised module recommendations, and an explicit caution on self-report reliability. No data is stored or transmitted.
