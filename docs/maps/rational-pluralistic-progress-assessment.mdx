## Rational → Pluralistic Stage Progress Self-Assessment

This interactive tool helps you explore where you are in the developmental movement from Rational (Orange) toward Pluralistic (Green) meaning-making. Unlike trait-based self-assessments that ask "how empathic are you?", this tool uses **scenario-based questions** — concrete situations with behavioral response options — to reveal your actual meaning-making patterns.

The assessment has three sections:

- **Section A (Where Are You Now?):** 8 questions exploring your current relationship to achievement, meaning, autonomy, and your own worldview — identifying the characteristic signals of late-Orange development.
- **Section B (Exploring Pluralistic Capacities):** 12 scenario-based questions across five domains: empathy, contextual ethics, ecological awareness, inclusive dialogue, and relativism recognition.
- **Section C (Shadow Awareness):** 6 questions about the characteristic blind spots that can accompany pluralistic worldviews — the shadow side of the capacity being assessed.

At the end, you will receive a descriptive profile of your emerging capacities, consolidated strengths, and growing edges — plus personalized module recommendations. No data is stored or transmitted. No stage labels or scores are assigned.
