## Shadow Work Self-Assessment

This interactive tool helps you explore your relationship with shadow — the disowned, denied, or projected aspects of yourself that operate outside conscious awareness. Shadow is not a defect; it is inherent to human psychology. The capacity to recognize it is itself a developmental achievement.

The assessment has three sections:

- **Section A (Shadow Mechanisms):** 10 scenario-based questions exploring how five core shadow mechanisms — projection, repression, splitting, reaction formation, and introjection — may operate in your experience.
- **Section B (Developmental Line Shadow):** 8 questions identifying which developmental lines (emotional, interpersonal, cognitive, moral, spiritual, self, somatic, shadow awareness) carry the strongest shadow charge.
- **Section C (Spiritual Bypassing):** 12 items adapted from the spiritual bypassing inventory, exploring how spiritual practice may be used to avoid rather than integrate difficult material.

You will receive a descriptive profile, personalized module recommendations, and — if your responses suggest material that may benefit from facilitated support — a recommendation to consider working with a therapist or shadow work facilitator. No data is stored or transmitted.
