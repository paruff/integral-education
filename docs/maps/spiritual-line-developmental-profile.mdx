## Spiritual Line Developmental Profile Assessment

This interactive assessment explores the **structure** of your relationship to the sacred — not the content of your beliefs. It uses phenomenological prompts and scenario responses to support developmental self-reflection across Fowler-oriented patterns.

The assessment has three sections:

- **Section A (Spiritual Relationship Structure):** 8 open-ended prompts about the texture and quality of your relationship to ultimate reality.
- **Section B (Spiritual Challenge Scenarios):** 6 scenarios on doubt, tradition-questioning, interfaith encounter, and spiritual disorientation.
- **Section C (Practice and Tradition Relationship):** 5 questions about contemplative practice, commitment, depth, and discernment.

At the end, you will receive a structural spiritual profile, state-stage distinction guidance, and personalized module recommendations. No data is stored or transmitted.
