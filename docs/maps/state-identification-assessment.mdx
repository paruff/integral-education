# 🧭 State Identification Self-Assessment

## Overview

Learners frequently conflate **states** (temporary altered experiences) with **stages** (enduring developmental levels) — a confusion that AQAL theory directly addresses. This self-assessment helps you identify which state you are most frequently accessing in practice, receive a personalised recommendation for deepening, and understand how your current state access relates to your likely stage centre of gravity.

The assessment covers four state bands from the Wilberian state spectrum:

| State Band | Phenomenological Markers |
|---|---|
| **Gross** | Ordinary waking awareness; sensorimotor reality; body, breath, external sounds |
| **Subtle** | Inner luminosity; flowing energy; absorbed concentration; hypnagogic imagery |
| **Causal / Witness** | Open awareness; witnessing stillness; thoughts observed without identification |
| **Nondual** | Dissolution of subject-object boundary; seamless, boundless presence |

## Instructions

Answer each question based on your **actual recent practice experience**, not on how you hope your practice will develop. There are no right or wrong responses. The assessment takes approximately 5–8 minutes to complete.

## About the State Spectrum

### Gross State (Ordinary Waking)
The gross state is the default waking mode — sensorimotor reality, physical body, external sounds, ordinary thought. It is the necessary foundation for all other state access. Most contemplative traditions begin here with body-anchored or breath-focused practices.

### Subtle State (Inner Luminosity and Absorption)
The subtle state is characterised by inner luminosity, flowing energy, absorbed concentration, and dream-like imagery. It is accessed through extended sits, Yoga Nidra, hypnagogic threshold practices, and deep concentration (samatha). Subtle states are temporary and not identical to developmental stages, even when they are profound.

### Causal / Witness State (Open Awareness)
The causal or witnessing state is characterised by a stable, spacious background from which thoughts, feelings, and perceptions are observed without identification. It is accessed through open awareness meditation, self-inquiry, and noting practice. The witness is not a developmental endpoint — it too can be released into nondual presence.

### Nondual State (Seamless Presence)
The nondual state involves a dissolving of the ordinary subject-object boundary — a glimpse of awareness as the ground of experience rather than as a separate observer. It is pointed to by Dzogchen, Advaita, Zen, and other non-dual traditions. Glimpse quality and integration are distinct; frequent glimpses do not guarantee stable integration.

---

## State vs. Stage: An Important Distinction

A high score on causal or nondual state access does **not** indicate an advanced developmental stage. States are temporary and peak experiences available at many stage levels. Stages reflect enduring structural capacities that develop over time through lived experience, challenge, and integration.

Someone at an Amber stage centre of gravity can have profound nondual glimpses. Someone with a Green or Teal stage centre of gravity may primarily access gross-state awareness. The value of state work is in deepening access, stabilising integration, and understanding how states and stages interact — not in using state access as a proxy for developmental altitude.

## Safety Notes

This assessment involves self-reflective inquiry about contemplative experiences. It is **Tier 1 (low intensity — self-paced, no facilitator required)**.

- This tool is not a substitute for guidance from an experienced teacher in your contemplative tradition.
- If practice-related experiences are causing distress, confusion, or functional impairment, consult a qualified teacher or mental health professional.
- See the [State Development Safety Standard](../safety/state-development-safety-standard) for full contraindications and escalation guidance.
