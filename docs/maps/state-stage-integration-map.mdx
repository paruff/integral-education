## 🗺️ State × Stage Integration Reference Map

## Overview

One of Ken Wilber's most theoretically sophisticated contributions in *Integral Spirituality* (2006) and *The Religion of Tomorrow* (2017) is the demonstration that **the same state can be accessed by practitioners at any developmental stage, yet will be interpreted through radically different meaning-making lenses**. A practitioner who glimpses nondual awareness at an Amber stage centre of gravity may describe it as dissolving into God's love; the same glimpse encountered from an Orange centre of gravity may be decoded as a neurological anomaly or a peak flow state; from Green it may be received as an experience of deep ecological interdependence; and from Teal it may be integrated into a multi-perspectival, intersubjective map of consciousness itself.

This asymmetry has profound practical consequences. Two practitioners reporting "the same experience" may be using identical words to point at entirely different meaning structures. Conversely, two practitioners at the same developmental altitude may dismiss each other's reports as naïve or confused simply because their stage lenses render the shared state unrecognisable. Understanding this cross-matrix relationship protects against spiritual inflation (believing a state experience equals developmental advancement), spiritual bypassing (using state access to avoid stage-level shadow work), and inter-practitioner misunderstanding in peer learning and facilitation contexts.

The **state axis** of the matrix draws on the five-state spectrum used throughout this platform: gross waking, subtle (dream-like luminosity and energy), causal/formless (deep stillness), the witnessing position itself, and nondual. The **stage axis** uses four developmental altitudes — Amber (Mythic-Conventional), Orange (Rational-Achievement), Green (Pluralistic-Egalitarian), and Teal (Integral-Systemic) — corresponding to the developmental bands most active in adult learning populations engaging with this curriculum.

It is important to emphasise that the matrix cells describe **interpretive frameworks**, not states themselves. The gross state is not "better" at Teal than at Amber; it is simply interpreted through a richer conceptual apparatus. Similarly, a high-altitude stage does not guarantee superior state access. Growing Up (stage development) and Waking Up (state development) are relatively independent developmental streams that enrich one another when integrated, but neither reduces to the other.

This reference map is designed for learners who have completed foundational modules in both state access and stage awareness, as well as for facilitators and coaches who need a practical tool for holding multiple interpretive frameworks simultaneously in a learning or coaching conversation.

## The State × Stage Matrix

The table below describes how each state is typically **interpreted and integrated** at each developmental stage. Each cell is a 2–4 sentence description of the meaning-making pattern, not a prescription for how the state "should" be experienced. Real experience is always richer, messier, and more individual than any map cell can capture.

| State | **Amber** (Mythic-Conventional) | **Orange** (Rational-Achievement) | **Green** (Pluralistic-Egalitarian) | **Teal** (Integral-Systemic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gross (waking)** | Ordinary reality is sacred creation governed by divine order or natural law handed down through tradition. The body is a gift from God or a vessel of duty; sensory experience confirms the rightness of established roles and structures. Disruptions to the familiar are interpreted as threats to order or signs of moral failure. | Ordinary waking reality is the primary arena of rational agency, material achievement, and measurable progress. The body is a performance system; sensorimotor experience is data for optimisation. States that do not produce measurable results tend to be dismissed as unproductive. | The Green meaning-maker meets ordinary waking reality as a field of intersubjective resonance: co-created, culturally embedded, and perspectival. The body is read through somatic sensitivity rather than as a performance system, and ordinary awareness is the site of empathic presence, ecological attunement, and care-ethical responsiveness. Distress is processed relationally — through dialogue, community witnessing, and collaborative meaning-making — rather than analytically or privately. *Shadow:* every ordinary encounter is interpreted as confirming Green's worldview ("we are all fundamentally interconnected"), reinforcing the stage lens rather than testing it — a mild pre/trans colonisation of waking experience by Green's relational ontology. | The Teal meaning-maker holds gross waking reality simultaneously within multiple non-competing interpretive frames — a neurobiological system (It-quadrant), a stream of felt interiority (I-quadrant), a culturally co-constructed lifeworld (We-quadrant), and an adaptive subsystem within social and ecological structures (Its-quadrant) — without requiring any single frame to dominate. Crucially, the interpretive act itself is visible: the practitioner recognises that how they organise ordinary waking experience reflects a cognitive-stage structure, not simply the nature of reality. Ordinary embodied life is met with full engagement and epistemic lightness — acting from a chosen frame while holding its partiality. *Shadow:* the breadth of multiperspectival awareness can itself become a form of identification — the practitioner subtly locating 2nd Tier worth in the sophistication of their holding ("I can see from multiple frameworks simultaneously"), which insulates gross waking experience from genuine inquiry rather than opening it. |
| **Subtle (luminous/absorbed)** | Profound states of inner light, absorption, or visionary experience are interpreted as divine visitation, saintly grace, angelic communication, or answered prayer. They confirm the reality of the supernatural order and the favour of God or higher powers. Significance is received through mythic-religious interpretation and often mediated by a spiritual authority or sacred text. | Subtle states are decoded through a naturalistic lens: they are attributed to neurological events, flow states, right-hemisphere activity, or altered dopamine and serotonin dynamics. They may be valued as productivity tools (peak performance, creative insight) or as fascinating anomalies worth studying. Metaphysical interpretations are typically rejected or held with ironic distance. | Subtle states — luminosity, absorbed stillness, energetic aliveness — are received at Green as confirmation of dimensions beyond Orange's reductionism and are held through multiple frameworks simultaneously: somatic (the body opening or vibrating), relational-mystical (felt connection across distance or species), ecological (belonging to living systems), and transpersonal (contact with a spiritual depth rational culture excluded). These experiences are shared in community as gifts and validating stories. *Shadow:* because the subtle experience feels unmistakably real and beautiful, it is taken as experiential proof of Green's radical interconnection — "this proves we are all one" — borrowing the state's transrational quality to validate a pre-integral worldview, confusing Green's relational ontology with what the state itself discloses. | Subtle states — inner luminosity, energetic vitality, absorbed stillness — are held at Teal within multiple simultaneous interpretive frameworks: Buddhist vibrational phenomenology (prana, luminous display of rigpa), neurological correlates (altered arousal regulation, reduced default mode network activity, and brainwave shifts documented in contemplative neuroscience), phenomenological description (pure qualitative texture without conceptual overlay), and AQAL coordinate (interior-individual, accessible across all stage altitudes). No single framework is privileged; the Teal practitioner selects whichever lens is most useful to context — practice, teaching, peer dialogue, or scientific inquiry — without confusing map for territory. The interpretive act itself is tracked: noticing the moment of reaching for a framework is itself a kind of practice, revealing the meaning-making machinery that operates even within refined contemplative experience. *Shadow:* multiperspectival fluency may be co-opted by 2nd Tier identity — the subtle satisfaction of holding Buddhist, neuroscientific, and phenomenological lenses simultaneously becomes its own form of stage-level self-confirmation, subtler and harder to detect than Green's identification with any single framework, yet similarly insulating against the state's deeper invitation to loosen the interpreter. |
| **Causal / formless** | The formless or empty ground of deep stillness is rare, highly valued, and may be interpreted as the presence of the divine beyond all images — the dark night, the cloud of unknowing, the via negativa of mystical traditions. It may also provoke anxiety if it is experienced as loss of God's presence rather than pure presence itself. | Formless stillness is often unfamiliar territory and may initially be experienced as disorienting or "blank." It may be pathologised as a dissociative episode or dismissed as merely sleepiness. If explored, it tends to be approached empirically — as a state to be documented, reproduced, and explained in terms of default mode network quieting or trance. | Causal stillness is welcomed at Green as spacious openness and the dissolution of the defensive constructions the self maintains against relationship. It is most commonly interpreted through Buddhist emptiness (sunyata) held within a relational ethic ("what does this openness call me toward in community?"), indigenous traditions of receptive spaciousness, or somatic frameworks of ego-softening and embodied surrender. The Green lens is drawn to the causal state's apparent egalitarianism — the formless ground feels undivided, without rank. *Shadow:* the dissolution of distinctions in causal stillness is read as experiential proof that all distinctions — including developmental ones — are ultimately illusory, collapsing a genuine transrational insight (emptiness includes form) into a pre-integral flattening that reinforces Green's existing relativism. | The causal or formless state is recognised at Teal as a distinct, mappable zone on the state spectrum — the ground prior to subtle luminosity and prior to ordinary mental content — and can be held simultaneously through Buddhist emptiness philosophy (sunyata), Christian apophatic mysticism (via negativa, the cloud of unknowing), neuroscientific accounts of default mode network deactivation, and phenomenological description of pure apperception without object. Unlike the stage-specific monopoly of earlier altitudes, Teal holds causal stillness without requiring resolution into any single grammar; the practitioner is also aware that even "formlessness" is partly a stage-level interpretation of whatever is encountered in deep stillness. This awareness of the interpretive act is held lightly — not compulsively tracked — allowing genuine entry into stillness alongside metacognitive clarity. *Shadow:* continuous meta-commentary on frameworks can substitute for actually entering causal stillness, maintaining a subtle observer stance that prevents genuine dissolution into the formless, while the sophistication of the meta-level narration confirms 2nd Tier identity and creates a spiritually informed but functionally avoidant relationship to depth. |
| **Witness (turiya / pure awareness)** | The witnessing position — pure observing awareness prior to content — is rarely distinguished from the content it witnesses at this stage. If encountered, it may be interpreted as God watching through human eyes, or as the soul's native perspective. Mystical traditions at this altitude tend to describe the witness in personal, devotional terms rather than as an impersonal aware space. | The witness is approached analytically: it may be equated with meta-cognition, executive function, or the observer effect. Practitioners with strong mindfulness practice may intellectually map the witness but have difficulty sustaining identification with awareness rather than with thought. The instinct to explain or narrate the witnessing experience in third-person terms is strong. | At Green, the witnessing position is warmly received as respite from the relentless interpersonal demands of the pluralistic centre of gravity. Its non-judgmental, spacious quality resonates naturally with Green's ethic of unconditional positive regard. However, the stage lens colonises the awareness position: the witness is experienced not as impersonal aware space but as the experiential ground of empathy and universal acceptance — Green's social values imported into what AQAL treats as relatively content-free awareness. *Shadow:* the pre/trans confusion of witness with epistemic pluralism — the practitioner accesses non-judgmental witnessing and concludes that "all perspectives are equally valid," translating a transrational capacity into a stage-level philosophical position. | At Teal, the witnessing position is precisely located on the state spectrum as a stabilised, relatively impersonal awareness position that precedes both subject and object, accessible across developmental stages, and refracted through multiple valid interpretive lenses — Advaita sakshi (pure witness awareness), Advaita turiya, Buddhist sati-sampajañña (mindful clear-knowing), Husserl's phenomenological reduction, neuroscientific meta-awareness (prefrontal monitoring of experience), and the AQAL state-stage coordinate — without any one being privileged. The Teal practitioner is explicitly aware that labelling the experience "pure witness" is already a cognitive-stage move: the map of witnessing is not the witnessing itself. Practitioners can distinguish witness from meta-cognition, sustain it across ordinary activities, and notice when the witness congeals into a subtle subject resisting release into nondual. *Shadow:* the recognition that "all accounts of the witness are stage-dependent interpretations" can paradoxically consolidate a 2nd Tier self-sense as the sophisticated observer who knows this — which is itself a witness-stance claimed by the integral identity rather than genuinely relinquished, a refined version of the same identification the stage purports to see through. |
| **Nondual** | Nondual glimpses, when they occur, are typically interpreted through the available mythic-religious grammar: union with God, dissolution into the divine, bridal mysticism, or loss of the individual in the ocean of the Absolute. The interpretation is richly meaningful and often transformative. However, because the stage lens does not yet include a concept of awareness as impersonal ground, the nondual event tends to reinforce the devotional relationship rather than dissolve the subject-object structure in a stable way. | If nondual glimpses occur at an Orange centre of gravity, they are frequently bracketed as anomalous, described in hedged language ("a kind of oneness feeling"), or explained away as a neurological event. The stage's strong investment in rational self-authorship makes it uncomfortable to report the dissolution of the separate self. Nondual experiences are therefore either pathologised, privatised, or translated into motivational/flow-state language. | At Green, nondual experience is received with unusual openness — the pluralistic stage has already loosened the self-authoring boundaries Orange requires, so dissolution of the separate self feels less threatening and more welcome. Nondual glimpses are framed through ecological communion ("I dissolved into the web of life"), intersubjective resonance ("the boundary between me and others disappeared"), and mystical validation of relational ontology ("this proves everything IS connected"). These are phenomenologically genuine responses, and Green's somatic sensitivity gives its practitioners real receptivity to boundary dissolution. *Shadow:* the genuinely transrational glimpse is appropriated to confirm a pre-integral worldview — "all is one, therefore all perspectives are equally valid" — conflating non-separation with enforced epistemic egalitarianism, so nondual language proliferates as a communal ideal while the discriminating awareness needed to integrate the glimpse (distinguishing realisation from peak experience, holding developmental differences within non-separation) remains undeveloped. | At Teal, nondual state experiences are held with genuine multiperspectivalism and productive uncertainty about what the experience ultimately means — without translating the glimpse into any single tradition's grammar, the practitioner can simultaneously reference Dzogchen rigpa, Advaita sahaja samadhi, Zen mu, Cook-Greuter's construct-aware dissolution of meaning-making structures, and the AQAL nondual coordinate, treating each as a partial illumination of what resists final conceptualisation. Paradoxically, this willingness to dwell in productive uncertainty — to hold multiple interpretive frames without forcing resolution — may deepen rather than diminish the glimpse's transformative impact, since the compulsion to secure the experience within a known meaning-structure is itself released. The Teal practitioner also recognises that whatever "nondual" names in language is already a stage-level conceptualisation of what is, by definition, prior to conceptualisation — and rests with this recursive openness rather than resolving it prematurely. *Shadow:* the most sophisticated Teal risk is using multiperspectival non-attachment itself as a confirmation of 2nd Tier identity — "my nondual experience is more integral because I am not bound to any single framework" — which is a subtle form of identification operating at the level of meta-interpretation, and can function as a spiritually refined developmental narcissism that forecloses genuine integration by preserving an integral self-image precisely within the apparent absence of one. |

<Admonition type="note" title="🧑‍🏫 Facilitator Note: Using This Map in Coaching and Group Facilitation">

**Purpose of the map in practice:** This matrix is not a diagnostic tool. It does not tell you which stage a learner is "at" based on their state language. It is a *widening frame* — a way of holding the possibility that two practitioners describing the same state may be meaning very different things, and that both accounts are coherent within their respective stage lenses.

**In individual coaching:** When a client reports a profound state experience (a meditation insight, a peak moment, a felt sense of dissolution), use the matrix rows and columns as a gentle inquiry framework. Ask how they are making meaning of the experience: What does it mean to them? What does it confirm or challenge? What tradition or worldview informs their interpretation? These questions — not the matrix itself — surface the stage lens without labelling the person.

**In group facilitation:** When learners compare notes after a shared practice (e.g., a group sit or a structured reflection), use the matrix to normalise interpretive differences. Two people who sat in the same room, with the same instructions, may report radically different experiences. Naming the stage-lens effect — "we are all working from different interpretive frameworks, and all of them are valid at their level" — can significantly reduce the subtle competition and comparison that emerges in contemplative learning communities.

**Developmental ethics:** Never use this map to rank or dismiss a learner's experience. A profound devotional interpretation of a nondual glimpse (Amber) is not a lesser experience than a Teal meta-perspective on the same event. The map describes interpretation, not experience quality. Treat every stage lens with genuine respect.

**Contraindication:** This map is not appropriate for use in crisis situations, for learners experiencing state-related distress, or as a substitute for stage-level developmental coaching grounded in the [Instructional Design Protocol](../quality/instructional-design-protocol). If a learner's state experiences are causing significant confusion or distress, refer to the [State Development Safety Standard](../safety/state-development-safety-standard).

</Admonition>

## Safety Notes

This reference map involves conceptual engagement with altered states of consciousness and developmental frameworks. It is **Tier 1 (low intensity — intellectual and reflective, no active state induction)**.

- This map does not induce states and does not require active contemplative practice to use.
- If using this map alongside active state practices, follow the safety guidance in the relevant state modules (see State Development Modules above).
- If a learner is experiencing confusion, distress, or functional impairment related to state or stage work, refer to the [State Development Safety Standard](../safety/state-development-safety-standard) and, if appropriate, to a qualified teacher or mental health professional.
- Do not use stage labels (Amber, Orange, Green, Teal) in ways that demean, pathologise, or dismiss a learner's experience. All stage lenses reflect genuine coherence at their level of complexity.
- See the [Shadowwork Safety Standard](../safety/shadowwork-safety-standard) for additional guidance if state practice reveals shadow material.
