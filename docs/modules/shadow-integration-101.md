# 🧠 Learn

**Shadow** = the disowned aspects of self — qualities, impulses, and capacities that were deemed unacceptable and pushed out of conscious awareness.

Carl Jung identified the shadow as the storehouse of everything we have rejected about ourselves. Paradoxically, what we reject in ourselves, we often project onto others.

Key concepts:
- **Projection** — attributing your own disowned traits to other people
- **Trigger** — the emotional charge that signals a shadow is active
- **Integration** — consciously owning and relating to the disowned quality

The goal is not to eliminate shadow — it's to make the unconscious conscious.

---

# 🧘 Practice

**Duration:** 15–20 min

**Instructions:**
1. Track your emotional triggers for 24 hours
2. When triggered, write: *"What trait am I reacting to in this person?"*
3. Ask: *"Where does this trait exist in me, even slightly?"*
4. Sit quietly and acknowledge the disowned quality with compassion
5. Complete: *"I own that I can be _____ , and that's human."*

---

# 🔍 Reflect

- What trait in others triggered the strongest reaction?
- Where have you expressed that same trait, even subtly?
- What would it mean to fully own this quality?
- How did your body feel when you acknowledged the projection?

- Act consciously in the direction of a previously disowned quality
- Practice expressing a disowned trait appropriately (e.g., assertiveness if passivity is the mask)
