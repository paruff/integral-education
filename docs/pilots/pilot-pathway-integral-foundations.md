# Pilot Pathway A: Integral Foundations (4 Weeks)

Audience: adult learners building baseline integral literacy and practical transfer skills.

## Design Constraints

- Microlearning units are 3-5 minutes, one concept each.
- Every week includes retrieval at 24h, 72h, and 7d.
- Interleaving progression: week 1-2 uses AABBCC pattern, week 3-4 uses ABCABC pattern.

## Weekly Arc

| Week | Focus | AQAL Emphasis | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation and AQAL basics | Quadrants + levels | Formative checks + concept map |
| 2 | Lines, states, types in practice | Lines + states + types | Applied scenario analysis |
| 3 | Integrative problem solving | 4Q synthesis | Performance memo |
| 4 | Transfer to real context | Full AQAL stack | Capstone mini-case and reflection |

## Week 1 Example (Microlearning Sequence)

- Lesson 1 (3-5 min): Why partial perspectives fail.
- Lesson 2 (3-5 min): Four quadrants quick map.
- Lesson 3 (3-5 min): Levels as complexity, not labels.
- Practice (5 min): Quadrant tagging drill.
- Retrieval:
  - 24h: 5-item mixed recall check.
  - 72h: scenario-to-quadrant matching.
  - 7d: short applied reflection.

## Performance-Based Assessment

Task: Analyze a workplace challenge using all four quadrants and propose one intervention per quadrant.

Rubric dimensions:

- AQAL completeness
- Evidence quality of reasoning
- Feasibility of interventions
- Reflection and adaptation plan

## Facilitation Notes

- Keep examples workplace-relevant.
- Encourage discussion but prevent abstract drift.
- Require citation for non-trivial claims in reflections.
