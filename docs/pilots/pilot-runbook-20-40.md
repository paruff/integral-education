## Pilot Runbook (20-40 Adult Learners)

This runbook defines execution steps for human teams. Copilot can prepare materials but cannot execute participant operations.

## Ownership Boundary

- Copilot-preparable: scripts, templates, forms, analytics schemas, report drafts.
- Human-required: recruitment, consent handling, live facilitation, safety escalation, final interpretation.

## 1. Recruitment and Cohort Setup (Human Required)

- Target sample: 20-40 adult learners.
- Balance across experience level and availability.
- Track demographics only as needed for evaluation and ethics policy.

## 2. Consent and Screening (Human Required)

- Provide participant information sheet.
- Obtain informed consent.
- Administer readiness and contraindication screening for shadow pathway.
- Route ineligible participants to non-shadow pathway alternatives.

## 3. Pilot Timeline (Suggested)

- Week 0: onboarding, baseline survey, orientation.
- Week 1-4: pathway delivery.
- Week 5: delayed retention checks and debrief.

## 4. Data Capture Plan

- Learning metrics: retrieval scores, PBA rubric scores, completion rates.
- Engagement metrics: lesson completion latency, practice adherence, forum contributions.
- Safety metrics: incident count by severity, time to response, escalation outcomes.
- Experience metrics: qualitative reflections and post-pilot interviews.

## 5. Session Operations Checklist

- [ ] Facilitator roster and backup coverage confirmed.
- [ ] Safety protocol briefing completed.
- [ ] Escalation contacts active.
- [ ] Accessibility checks complete.
- [ ] Session logs and incident form links available.

## 6. Risk Controls

- Run shadow activities with explicit stop rules.
- Keep high-intensity exercises out of self-guided mode.
- Review incident logs within 24 hours.
- Pause pilot if repeated unresolved safety incidents occur.

## 7. Closeout Requirements

- Conduct post-pilot survey and optional interviews.
- Complete revision report template.
- Convert recommendations into backlog issues with ownership tags.
