# 🧭 Amber → Rational (QuickStart)

**Goal:** Transition from rule-based, authority-driven thinking to evidence-based, critical reasoning.

**Time commitment:** 2–4 weeks (20–30 min/day)

---

## Step 1: Rules → Evidence

**Module:** [Cognitive Bias 101](../modules/cognitive-bias-101)

Learn to distinguish between inherited assumptions and evidence-based conclusions.

- Identify one assumption per day you inherited from authority figures
- Ask: *What evidence supports this? What evidence contradicts it?*
- **Goal:** Catch and name 3 cognitive biases in your own thinking this week

---

## Step 2: Systems Thinking

**Module:** [Systems Thinking 101](../modules/systems-thinking-101)

See how parts connect to form wholes.

- Map one system you're part of (family, organization, ecosystem)
- Identify feedback loops and unintended consequences
- **Goal:** Describe one system with at least 3 interconnected elements and 1 feedback loop

---

## Outcome

By completing this QuickStart, you will have developed:

- ✅ Critical thinking skills
- ✅ Evidence-based reasoning
- ✅ Awareness of cognitive biases
- ✅ Basic systems perspective
