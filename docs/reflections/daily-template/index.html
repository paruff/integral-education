🪞 Daily Reflection Template

Use this template daily to track your growth across all quadrants.

Date: _______________
Duration: _______________

What happened?​

Describe 1–3 significant events, interactions, or moments from today.

What did I feel?​

Use specific emotion words (avoid "good/bad/stressed"). Aim for 3+ distinct emotions.

What did I think?​

What patterns of thought were most active? Any biases noticed?

What did I do?​

What actions did you take? Any reactive or consciously chosen behaviors?

What did I learn?​

One insight, realization, or shift in perspective from today.

AQAL Tagging​

Tag today's experience using the AQAL framework.

  • Quadrant(s): I / We / It / Its
  • Line(s): cognitive / emotional / somatic / interpersonal / moral / spiritual
  • Level: (Where was I operating from today?)
  • State: (What states of consciousness were most present?)
  • Type: (Any type-related patterns — personality, cultural?)

Tomorrow's Intention​

One small, concrete action to embody what you learned today.
