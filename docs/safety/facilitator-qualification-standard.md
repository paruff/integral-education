# Facilitator Qualification Standard

This standard defines the minimum qualifications, supervision ratios, certification pathways, and incident-response requirements for facilitators delivering Tier 1–3 shadowwork and reflective practices on this platform.

> **[HUMAN-REQUIRED]** All tables marked `[PLACEHOLDER]` must be completed by qualified humans before this standard is authoritative. Clinical, legal, and ethics review is required before any facilitator is certified or any Tier 2–3 session is delivered. See backlog issue E4-2.

> **Boundary:** This document provides the governance framework and acceptance criteria. Actual certification decisions, roster population, and availability verification are human-required actions and cannot be delegated to Copilot.

---

## 1. Scope and Purpose

This standard applies to:

- All facilitators delivering platform-hosted shadowwork or reflective practice sessions.
- All supervision arrangements for Tier 2 and Tier 3 delivery.
- All incident-response personnel listed on the platform safety roster.

This standard does **not** apply to purely informational or knowledge-transfer content that carries no reflective practice component.

## 2. Qualification Minimums by Tier

### 2.1 Tier 1 — Low Intensity (Self-Guided Support)

A facilitator is not required for Tier 1 delivery. If a facilitator is present or provides asynchronous support:

| Requirement | Minimum Standard | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Platform safety orientation | Completed (self-paced module, ≥ 2 hours) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Shadow protocol overview | Completed (recommended, not required) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Asynchronous response capability | Available within 24 hours for distress signals | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Contraindications familiarity | Able to identify Tier 1 stop-rule triggers | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |

### 2.2 Tier 2 — Moderate Intensity (Facilitator-Supervised)

| Requirement | Minimum Standard | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Platform safety training | Completed (≥ 4 hours, documented) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Shadow protocol training | Completed (≥ 8 hours, documented) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Supervised facilitation hours | ≥ 10 hours documented at Tier 1 or above | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Observed practice evaluation | At least 1 observed session with written evaluation | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Consent and contraindications delivery | Able to present informed consent language, review contraindications, and verify entry criteria before each session | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Incident response certification | Completed; renewal annually | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Live debrief capability | Demonstrated in observed evaluation | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Clinical supervisor access | Supervisor identified; consultation available within 48 hours | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` assign |
| Co-facilitator | Recommended for groups ≥ 4 participants | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |

### 2.3 Tier 3 — High Intensity (Facilitator-Led Only)

| Requirement | Minimum Standard | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Platform safety training | Completed (≥ 4 hours, documented) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Shadow protocol training | Completed (≥ 20 hours, documented) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Supervised facilitation hours | ≥ 40 hours documented (≥ 10 at Tier 2 or higher) | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Observed practice evaluations | At least 2 observed sessions at Tier 2 with written evaluations | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Consent and contraindications delivery | Able to present full Tier 3 informed consent, clinical self-report screen review, and individual pre-clearance for each participant | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Incident response certification | Completed; renewal annually | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Live debrief capability | Demonstrated; debrief script on file | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |
| Clinical supervisor access | Required; same-day or on-call consultation available | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` assign |
| Co-facilitator | Required for groups; required for intensive formats | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` assign |
| Emergency response training | Basic mental health first aid or equivalent | `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` verify |

## 3. Supervision Ratios

| Tier | Maximum Participants per Facilitator | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | No mandatory cap; support is asynchronous | Facilitator monitors distress signals; no live ratio required |
| Tier 2 | 1 facilitator : ≤ 6 participants | Co-facilitator strongly recommended for groups ≥ 4 |
| Tier 3 | 1 facilitator : ≤ 3 participants | Co-facilitator required for groups ≥ 2; solo Tier 3 delivery is prohibited |

> **`[HUMAN-REQUIRED]`** Supervision ratios above are proposed defaults. Final ratios must be approved by the Safety Lead and Clinical Advisor before pilot delivery.

## 5. Certification Pathway

### 5.1 Certification Process

Certification decisions are `[HUMAN-REQUIRED]` and must follow this sequence:

1. **Application:** Facilitator submits training records, references, and documented facilitation hours.
2. **Training verification:** Safety Lead reviews and verifies all prerequisite training documentation.
3. **Observed evaluation:** At least one live or recorded session reviewed by a certified evaluator.
4. **Clinical advisor sign-off:** For Tier 3 candidates, Clinical Advisor reviews and approves.
5. **Certification decision:** Safety Lead issues approval or denial with written rationale.
6. **Roster entry:** Certified facilitator is added to the official roster with tier, expiration date, and assigned supervisor.

### 5.2 Certification Criteria Summary

| Criterion | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Training hours verified | ≥ 8 hours shadow protocol | ≥ 20 hours shadow protocol |
| Supervised hours documented | ≥ 10 hours | ≥ 40 hours (≥ 10 at Tier 2+) |
| Observed evaluations passed | ≥ 1 | ≥ 2 at Tier 2 |
| Incident response cert current | Yes | Yes |
| Clinical advisor approval | Not required | Required |
| Safety Lead sign-off | Required | Required |

### 5.3 Certification Expiration and Renewal

| Tier | Initial Certification Validity | Renewal Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 2 | 12 months | Annual recertification: continuing education + observed session |
| Tier 3 | 12 months | Annual recertification: continuing education + observed session + clinical advisor re-approval |

> **`[HUMAN-REQUIRED]`** Lapsed certification requires full re-evaluation before resuming Tier 2 or Tier 3 delivery.

### 7.2 Escalation Pathway

The following escalation sequence applies when a participant exhibits acute distress, a stop-rule trigger, or a safety event during any session:

```
1. Facilitator activates stop rule → applies grounding protocol
2. Facilitator notifies Safety Lead (within 15 minutes of event)
3. Safety Lead assesses severity:
   a. Low severity (contained, resolved) → log incident; no further escalation
   b. Moderate severity (unresolved distress) → notify Clinical Advisor within 1 hour
   c. High severity (acute crisis, risk of harm) → notify Escalation Officer immediately;
      contact emergency services if imminent risk to life
4. Clinical Advisor or Escalation Officer determines follow-up care pathway
5. Incident documented in safety log within 24 hours
6. Post-incident debrief with facilitation team within 48 hours
```

> See [Shadowwork Safety Standard](shadowwork-safety-standard) for full stop-rule and grounding protocol details.

### 7.3 Communication Protocols

| Event Type | Primary Channel | Backup Channel | Documentation Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine distress signal (Tier 1, async) | Platform message | Email | Session log note |
| Stop-rule activated (Tier 2) | Direct call to Safety Lead | Text/Signal | Incident form within 24 hours |
| Stop-rule activated (Tier 3) | Direct call to Safety Lead + Clinical Advisor | Text/Signal | Incident form within 24 hours |
| Acute crisis / life-safety event | Escalation Officer + emergency services | Any available | Incident form within 24 hours; post-incident review within 48 hours |
| Facilitator concern (non-participant) | Email to Safety Lead | Direct message | Optional unless recurring |
