Shadowwork Safety Standard

This standard defines safety tiers, delivery constraints, and facilitator requirements for shadow-related practices.

Scope and Boundary​

  • This platform provides educational and developmental practices.
  • It does not provide therapy or clinical treatment.
  • Any severe distress signal requires immediate escalation to qualified human support.

Tier Model​

TierIntensityTypical PracticesDelivery Rule
Tier 1LowProjection spotting, trigger journaling, cognitive reframing promptsSelf-guided allowed with safety prompts
Tier 2ModerateStructured parts dialogue, role reversal, guided interpersonal repairFacilitator-supervised recommended
Tier 3HighDeep trauma-adjacent narratives, intense emotional processing protocolsFacilitator-led only; no self-guided mode

Entry Criteria​

Required before any shadow practice:

  • Explicit informed consent acknowledgment.
  • Baseline self-regulation skill completion.
  • Distress check-in completed.
  • Awareness of stop rules and grounding options.

Stop Rules​

Immediately stop activity when any condition is present:

  • Escalating panic, dissociation, or inability to orient.
  • Repeated inability to self-regulate with provided grounding.
  • Expression of risk to self or others.

Grounding and Fallback Protocol​

  • Offer immediate grounding menu (breath, sensory orientation, movement reset).
  • Shift to Tier 1 alternatives.
  • Record incident and notify facilitator channel when applicable.

Escalation Protocol​

  • Tier 1 incidents: document and monitor.
  • Tier 2 incidents: notify facilitator and require follow-up check.
  • Tier 3 incidents: immediate handoff to qualified human support pathway.

Facilitator Requirements​

RequirementTier 1Tier 2Tier 3
Platform safety trainingRequiredRequiredRequired
Shadow protocol trainingRecommendedRequiredRequired
Supervision hours documentedOptionalRequiredRequired
Incident response certificationOptionalRecommendedRequired
Live debrief capabilityOptionalRequiredRequired

Session Design Requirements​

  • Always include pre-brief, active practice, and debrief.
  • Keep novice sessions short and low intensity.
  • Never force disclosure; participation alternatives must exist.
  • Include post-session stabilization and integration prompt.

Data Handling for Sensitive Reflection​

  • Private by default.
  • Learner controls sharing permissions.
  • Use minimum retention period necessary.
  • Restrict access to authorized facilitation roles only.

Launch Readiness Checklist​

  • Tier classification assigned.
  • Consent and contraindications present.
  • Stop rules visible in activity UI.
  • Grounding and fallback alternatives tested.
  • Escalation path verified with human owners.
